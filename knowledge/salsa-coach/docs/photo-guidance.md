# Photo guidance for dancers

- Plain, light wall; dark clothes (or the reverse). Avoid a patterned backdrop and mirrors.
- Whole body (or both dancers) in frame, with some wall around you; camera at chest height; same spot and distance every time so attempts are comparable.
- Use the self-timer (3/5/10 s) and hold the shape for a beat.
- Even light from the front; strong side light makes the floor shadow join the silhouette.
- If "No dancer found": Manual threshold slider, or GrabCut on.
