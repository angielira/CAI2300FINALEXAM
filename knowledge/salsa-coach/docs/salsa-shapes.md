# Salsa shapes worth practising

Sources: dance-teaching practice and `docs/06-salsa-shape-matching.md` (domain knowledge; no single official source).

- **Dip:** the leader supports the follower as she arches back; judged by depth, a long line from head to supporting foot, and a stable frame. Practise slowly, with spotting.
- **Ending pose:** the final shape on the last beat: often an arm presentation or a lean. Clean lines matter more than height.
- **Shine styling:** solo pose during footwork: arm placement (one high, one framing), a pointed or crossed foot.
- **Cross-body lead finish** and **hammerlock**: partnered positions with a clear silhouette of arms.
- What silhouettes can't judge: timing, musicality, connection, weight transfer, spotting on turns.
