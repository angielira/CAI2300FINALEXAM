# Example coach tips (as shipped in js/app.js)

| Situation | Tip |
|---|---|
| score ≥ 85 | ¡Qué bueno! That is very close to your reference. |
| 70–84 | Close. The shape is there; polish the details. |
| 50–69 | Recognisable. Keep practising this one. |
| < 50 | Quite a different shape. Check the reference you picked, and that your whole body is in the frame. |
| aspect > 1.12 × reference | Your shape is taller and narrower than the reference. In a dip, go lower; in styling, open your arms wider. |
| aspect < 0.89 × reference | Your shape is lower and wider than the reference. Lift through the chest and stack your frame. |
| solidity +0.05 | You look more compact: extend your arms and pointed foot to open the line. |
| solidity -0.05 | Your limbs reach further out than the reference. Bring the styling arm a little closer. |
