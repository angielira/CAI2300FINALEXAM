# Example coach tips (as shipped in js/app.js)

| Situation | Tip |
|---|---|
| score ≥ 85 | Brava! That is very close to your reference. |
| 70–84 | Close. The line is there; refine the details. |
| 50–69 | Recognisable. Keep working on this position. |
| < 50 | Quite a different position. Check the reference you picked, and that your whole body is in the frame. |
| aspect > 1.12 × reference | Your line is taller and narrower than the reference: extend the working leg and the arms further out. |
| aspect < 0.89 × reference | Your shape is lower and wider than the reference: pull up through the standing leg and lengthen the spine. |
| solidity +0.05 | You look more compact: extend fully through the working leg and point the foot. |
| solidity -0.05 | Your limbs reach further out than the reference: bring the arms back into their rounded position. |
| recognition | "Looks most like: Arabesque · 75%" when the best pose scores ≥ 60 and wins by ≥ 10; otherwise "Closest pose (not sure)". |
