# Ballet poses in the gallery

Sources: classical ballet vocabulary as taught in the Vaganova, Cecchetti and RAD syllabi (domain knowledge) · American Ballet Theatre ballet dictionary: https://www.abt.org/explore/learn/ballet-dictionary/ (not fetched in the build session; re-verify definitions there) · measured on `samples/` with `tests/pipeline_check.py`.

- **Arabesque:** supporting leg straight, working leg extended straight behind; arms prolong the line (one forward, one to the side).
- **Penché:** an arabesque that tips forward, the working leg rising as the torso lowers.
- **Attitude derrière / devant:** working leg lifted behind / in front with the knee bent and held higher than the foot (behind) or the shin reaching forward (front); one arm usually overhead.
- **Développé devant / à la seconde:** the working leg unfolds to full extension in front / to the side at or above hip height.
- **Écarté:** body at an oblique angle, working leg extended to the side and up, torso tilted away, arm raised on the working-leg side.
- **Retiré (passé):** working foot drawn up to the supporting knee; here with arms rounded overhead in fifth (en haut).
- **First position, bras bas:** heels together, feet turned out; arms low and rounded.
- **Fifth position, en haut:** feet crossed and turned out; arms rounded overhead.
- **Second position plié:** feet apart, knees bent over the toes; arms open in second.
- **Tendu devant / à la seconde:** the working foot slides out to a point on the floor, front / side, leg fully stretched.
- **Fourth position croisé:** one foot in front of the other, crossed; one arm up, one to the side.
- **Grand jeté:** a big jump with the legs split front and back in the air.
- **Révérence:** the bow at the end of class or a performance; supporting leg straight, the other pointed behind, torso inclining forward, arms opening low.
- What silhouettes can't judge: turnout, hip placement, épaulement, timing, musicality.
