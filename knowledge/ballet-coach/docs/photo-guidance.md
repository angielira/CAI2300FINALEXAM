# Photo guidance for dancers

Sources: dance-teaching practice (domain knowledge) · `docs/06-ballet-pose-matching.md` · measured on `samples/` with `tests/pipeline_check.py`

- Plain, light wall; dark leotard and tights (or the reverse). Avoid the studio mirror and a patterned backdrop. A tutu or skirt is fine: it becomes part of the outline.
- Whole body in frame with some wall around it, including pointed feet and fingertips; camera at hip height; same spot and distance every time so attempts are comparable.
- Shoot side-on to the line you are showing: arabesque and attitude from the side, à la seconde and second position from the front.
- Use the self-timer (3/5/10 s) and hold the pose for a count.
- If "No dancer found": Manual threshold slider, or GrabCut on.
